# How to Reset Your Password

## Purpose

This guide shows you how to reset your password if you have forgotten it or cannot sign in.

## Before You Start

- You must have access to the email address or username linked to your account.
- You must be able to open your email inbox, including the spam or junk folder.

## Steps

1. Go to the sign-in page of the website or app.
2. Click **Forgot password?** (this may also appear as **Reset password** or **Can't sign in?**).
3. Enter the email address or username linked to your account.
4. Click **Submit** or **Continue**.
5. Open your email inbox and find the password reset message.
   - The email usually arrives within one to two minutes.
   - If you do not see it, check your spam or junk folder.
6. Open the email and click the **reset link**.

> **Note:** Reset links expire, often within 15 to 60 minutes. If your link has expired, repeat steps 1 to 5.

7. On the reset page, enter your new password.
8. Re-enter the new password to confirm it.
9. Click **Submit** or **Reset password**.
10. Return to the sign-in page and log in with your new password.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Reset email did not arrive | Check spam or junk folder. Wait 5 minutes. Request a new link. |
| Reset link expired | Return to the sign-in page and request a new link. |
| New password rejected | Check the on-screen password requirements and try again. |
| Still cannot sign in | Confirm you are using the new password, not the old one. Contact support. |
